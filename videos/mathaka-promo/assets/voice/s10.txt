Then each pill is measured, not guessed. You place it on a printed mat, with four markers. Plain TypeScript finds the markers, flattens the perspective, and turns pixels into millimetres. It balances the colour on a grey patch, cuts out the pill, and measures its size, shape, colour, two tone halves, and score line. Nineteen point two, by nine point two millimetres, in about two hundred milliseconds. The same code runs on the phone, in the browser, and in the tests.
